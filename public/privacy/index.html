Privacy

Effective 23 October 2026

dalgo.io is a static documentation site. It has no accounts and no advertising, and it does not sell data. This page says what happens when you visit it and the one choice you control: analytics.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to measure traffic. Google Signals and ad personalization are switched off, and we set no user ID and send no personal data.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or the United Kingdom, analytics storage is denied by default: no analytics cookie is set until you choose Accept in the banner, and none is set if you choose Decline. Elsewhere, analytics storage is allowed by default and no banner is shown. The Google tag still loads on every page; while storage is denied, Google Analytics may receive cookieless pings that include the page address, referrer, browser details and IP address, but sets no cookie and stores no identifier on your device. The banner appears based on the country code that Cloudflare, which hosts this site, reports for your request.

When analytics storage is allowed, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_<id>) and receives the page address, the referring page, browser and device information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

Your choice

Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage, key ga-consent). You can change it at any time below.
