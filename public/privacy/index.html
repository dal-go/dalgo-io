Privacy

Effective 23 October 2026

dalgo.io is a static documentation site. It has no accounts and no advertising, and it does not sell data. This page says what happens when you visit it and the one choice you control: analytics.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to measure traffic. Google Signals and ad personalization are switched off, we set no user ID, and we send no personal data beyond what Google Analytics collects automatically (described below).

If you visit from the European Economic Area (including its overseas territories), Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, Gibraltar or the Crown Dependencies, nothing is sent to Google and the Google Analytics script is not even loaded until you choose Accept in the banner. If you choose Decline, it does not load (or stops) and any analytics cookies already set on this site are deleted. Elsewhere, Google Analytics loads normally and no banner is shown. Your region is taken from the country code that Cloudflare, which hosts this site, reports for your request; if it cannot be determined, you are treated as being in the EEA or UK.

When Google Analytics runs, it sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_<id>, kept for up to two years) and receives the page address, the referring page, browser and device information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

In the EEA and UK the legal basis is your consent. We keep Google Analytics event data for no longer than 14 months.

Your choice

Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage, key dalgo.analytics-consent.v1). You can change it at any time below.
